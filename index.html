Rise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Rise: Motivational Wallpapers ("Rise", "the app") is published by kahn.AI Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: Rise does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Rise has no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything from your device.

Information stored on your device

A few settings are saved only on your phone so the app remembers them: the wallpapers you mark as favourites, whether the daily quote notification is on, the time you chose for it, and whether the app should change your wallpaper each morning. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions

  • Set wallpaper: to apply the wallpaper you choose to your home and/or lock screen.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later, optional): to show one quote each morning at the time you choose.
  • Run at startup: to re-schedule the daily quote after your phone restarts.
  • Storage (Android 9 and earlier only): to save a wallpaper to your gallery when you tap Save.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Android's share sheet opens and you choose where the image goes. Rise does not see or keep what you share.

Children

Rise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
